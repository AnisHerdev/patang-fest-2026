# Video Prompt

## Prompt
Create a 30–40 second vertical Instagram video promoting a college fest called “PATANG 2026”.

**Hook (first 3 seconds):**
 Start with fast cuts of colorful kites flying and Holi color bursts, bold on-screen text:
 “RVU, are you ready to fly?”
 Upbeat groovy music starts immediately.

**Main Content:**
 Show animated scenes of:
All-night Hackathon (laptops, night sky)


Ideathon & Social Experiments (ideas, people interaction)


Guinness World Record attempt with a crowd forming a Rubik’s Cube portrait


**Highlight Text Overlays:**
2 Days. Endless Energy.
January 14–15, 2026 | RV University
Big Events. Bigger Vibes.

**Ending:**
 Festive kite flying into the sky with text:
 “PATANG 2026 🪁 Don’t Miss It.”
 Add a quick “Register Now” call-to-action.

**Style:**
 Bright, clean, Holi-inspired colors, smooth transitions, modern college-fest vibe.
